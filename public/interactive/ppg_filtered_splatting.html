Click inside the quadtree to splat one radiance sample.
The square is the directional domain of one SD-tree leaf (cylindrical coordinates). The quadtree is built with PPG's rule: a node is subdivided while it holds more than 1% of the total flux of a synthetic radiance function. Cell colour shows the learned radiance density (deposited energy ÷ cell area). Switching mode replays the same samples, so the three schemes can be compared directly. Filter footprint = the size of the leaf the sample fell into, centred on the sample. In this demo, the part of a footprint that falls outside the square is renormalised away; that border handling is a simplification of the demo.
