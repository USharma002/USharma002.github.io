Topology Math

The Alpha Complex

The visualization represents the shape of data by placing balls of radius α (alpha) at each point. As α increases, the balls grow and intersect, forming connections.

Vietoris-Rips Complex

Used here for real-time performance. We connect points u and v if their balls intersect.

d(u, v) ≤ 2α

A k-simplex (triangle/tetrahedron) is formed if all pairs of vertices in the set are within distance 2α.

Homology

H₀: Connected Components (clusters).
H₁: Loops (holes/tunnels).
H₂: Voids (enclosed pockets).

Look for persistent holes (loops) in the Stanford Bunny as the "balls" merge.
