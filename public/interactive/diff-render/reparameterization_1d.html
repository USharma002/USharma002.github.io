Move Step Boundary ($\pi$): π = 0.20
Why Naïve AD Fails: Sample locations $x_i$ are fixed constants. As boundary $\pi$ moves, $x_i$ doesn't change position, so $\frac{\partial}{\partial \pi}[\mathbb{1}_{[\pi,\infty]}(x_i) g(x_i)] = 0$. Monte Carlo misses the moving boundary!
Why Reparameterization Works: We set sample positions $x_i(\pi) = y_i + \pi$ relative to $\pi$. As $\pi$ shifts, samples slide smoothly along $g(x)$, capturing the non-zero derivative $\frac{\partial}{\partial \pi} g(y_i + \pi) = g'(x_i)$.
